# Instituto Renacer Puebla — Brand & Website Direction

## 1. Brand essence

Instituto Renacer Puebla should be presented as a warm, professional and human environment for older adults, with a clear focus on **quality of life, prevention, independence and comprehensive wellbeing**.

The website should not feel like a hospital, a generic nursing-home template, or a cold healthcare institution. It should communicate that aging can remain active, connected, dignified and meaningful.

### Core idea
**Renacer is not only about care. It is about helping older adults preserve quality of life, autonomy, connection and wellbeing.**

### Emotional promise to families
The main website visitor is usually a family member evaluating whether Renacer is the right place for someone they love.

The site should progressively answer:

> **“Can I trust these people with someone I love?”**

The response should come through clarity, real evidence, professional care, human warmth, photography, transparent services and a simple invitation to visit.

---

## 2. Positioning

Renacer combines two dimensions:

### Human
- companionship
- dignity
- belonging
- purpose
- emotional wellbeing
- family reassurance
- everyday quality of life

### Professional
- multidisciplinary attention
- individualized plans
- nursing and medical follow-up
- rehabilitation / physical activity
- nutrition
- cognitive stimulation
- psychological support
- prevention before dependence

The website should balance both. It should never become so emotional that it loses credibility, nor so clinical that it loses warmth.

---

## 3. Brand message hierarchy

### Main promise
**Quality of life and independence in later adulthood.**

### Family-facing promise
**Your family member will be cared for, accompanied and supported while continuing to live an active, connected and meaningful daily life.**

### Professional promise
**Multidisciplinary, personalized support built around the real needs of each person.**

### Strategic philosophy
**Prevention before dependence.**

---

## 4. The 6 pillars

The strongest framework in the current brand communication is the set of six pillars that support healthy aging.

These should be treated as a **signature philosophy section**, not as a generic service grid.

### 1. Rendimiento cognitivo
Estimulación de la memoria, atención y funciones ejecutivas.

### 2. Ejercicio funcional
Fortalecimiento del cuerpo, movilidad e independencia en actividades diarias.

### 3. Nutrición inteligente
Alimentación personalizada y apoyo al bienestar nutricional y metabólico.

### 4. Acompañamiento y conexión
Vínculos, convivencia, sentido de pertenencia y propósito.

### 5. Equilibrio emocional
Salud emocional, manejo del estrés y tranquilidad mental.

### 6. Descanso y recuperación
Calidad del sueño y recuperación física y mental.

### Important distinction
**Pillars = what Renacer believes creates quality of life.**

**Services = what Renacer provides to support those pillars.**

Do not mix these concepts into one repetitive icon grid.

## 6. Voice and copy

### Tone
- warm
- clear
- reassuring
- respectful
- professional
- human
- optimistic without exaggeration

### Avoid
- infantilizing older adults
- guilt-based messaging to families
- fear-based selling
- exaggerated medical claims
- generic phrases that could belong to any residence
- excessive use of “abuelitos”
- sterile or bureaucratic language
- sentimental clichés

### Prefer language such as
- adultos mayores
- residentes
- familias
- acompañamiento
- bienestar
- independencia
- convivencia
- vida cotidiana
- atención personalizada
- calidad de vida
- envejecimiento activo

### Existing brand idea worth preserving
> “No se trata de agregar años a la vida, sino vida a los años.”

Use it only if appropriate in the final content hierarchy; do not repeat it everywhere.

## 7. Primary website audience

The main decision-maker is usually not the future resident.

Primary visitors are likely to be:
- sons and daughters
- grandchildren
- spouses / relatives
- guardians / family decision-makers

They are evaluating safety, quality of care, quality of life, professionalism, facilities, daily routine, trust and how easy it is to stay connected.

### Main conversion goal
**Agenda una visita.**

A visit should feel like a low-pressure next step, not an aggressive sales funnel.

Useful secondary actions can include:
- Conoce Renacer
- Habla con nosotros
- Ver instalaciones
- WhatsApp

## 8. Visual identity

Use the supplied brand assets as the source of truth.

### Color palette
- `#0A402C` — deep green
- `#85936A` — muted olive green
- `#B2B277` — soft olive
- `#D7DAB7` — light sage
- `#FFEFCA` — warm cream
- `#FFF6E1` — soft warm ivory
- `#4D4D4D` — neutral / secondary text

Use the exact values from `COLORES Y TIPOGRAFÍA.pdf` when implementing.

### Typography
The supplied typography sheet includes:
- **Nunito Sans**
- **Inter**

The sheet indicates Medium, Semibold, Bold and Extrabold weights across the system. Use the typography sheet and current brand examples to determine hierarchy. Avoid introducing a third family unless necessary.

### Logo system
The assets include multiple logo and imagotype variants in PNG and SVG. Prefer SVG for the website when appropriate.

Use logo variants intentionally based on background contrast. Do not redraw or alter the mark.

## 9. Photography and image treatment

The supplied promotional images show the intended emotional direction:
- older adults shown with dignity and personality
- close human connection
- warmth and natural expressions
- real-life rather than institutional feeling
- family/caregiver relationships

The website should use photography as evidence and emotional context, not as decoration.

### Preferred image roles
- Hero: one strong, calm image with room for text
- Pillars / philosophy: editorial pairing of text and imagery
- Daily life: authentic activity / interaction photography
- Family reassurance: connection between generations
- Facilities: real spaces whenever available

### Avoid
- fake AI-generated residents
- generic hospital stock photos
- staged medical imagery unless relevant
- excessive cutout collages
- using every supplied image simply because it exists

---

## 10. Web design character

The site should feel:
- warm
- editorial
- calm
- contemporary
- established
- premium but not luxurious
- residential rather than clinical
- intentionally designed rather than template-driven

### Avoid the typical AI/SaaS look
- giant gradient blobs
- glassmorphism
- excessive rounded cards
- glowing elements
- identical three-column sections repeated everywhere
- random icons above every paragraph
- huge empty hero copy
- excessive shadows
- excessive animation
- arbitrary stock art

Use whitespace, typography, photography and layout rhythm as the main design tools.

---

## 11. Recommended homepage narrative

The homepage should move through:

**Emotion → Understanding → Philosophy → Proof → Daily life → Trust → Visit / Contact**

A strong structure could be:

1. Header / navigation
2. Human hero + clear family-facing value proposition
3. Short trust / quick-understanding strip
4. Introduction to Renacer
5. Signature “6 pilares” section
6. Services / professional support
7. Daily life / activities
8. Facilities / environment
9. Team / multidisciplinary care, if verified content exists
10. Testimonials, only if real testimonials exist
11. FAQ / family reassurance
12. Strong “Agenda una visita” CTA
13. Location + contact
14. Footer

Do not force all sections if the actual content is not available.

---

## 12. UX principles

- Design first for the family decision-maker.
- Keep navigation simple.
- Make the next step obvious.
- Explain what Renacer actually does early.
- Use readable type and strong contrast.
- Avoid small gray text.
- Make WhatsApp / phone contact easy, especially on mobile.
- Ensure tap targets are generous.
- Use semantic HTML and accessible components.
- Do not invent stats, awards, certifications, testimonials or years of experience.

---

When building the website, use this priority:

1. Files in `/assets`
2. This brand guide
3. Verified client content supplied in the repository

5. General design judgment
